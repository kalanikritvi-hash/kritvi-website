Research Scholar

  • Selected as one of 20 students at Barnard for the research and leadership programme focused on global development and impact-driven research.
  • Reached out to and surveyed 36 Indian MSMEs on bank versus non-bank lending; both groups grew after borrowing, but the degree differed by lender type.
  • Bank borrowers more often reported strong turnover growth (27% vs. 0%) and added jobs (49% vs. 17%) than non-bank borrowers.

Behavioral Highlights

Short examples from my experience that I draw on in interviews.

Leadership

Founded From Stardust with no team and built it to 30 editors across writing, design and outreach, with section editors so the publication did not depend on me. Later led a second team of 15 at Hubble using the same structure. Together the two magazines published more than ten issues and reached about 2,000 followers.

Initiative

When Utopia's schools needed a water tank and books with no budget, I cold-emailed 15 businesses with a specific ask and cost, prioritizing founders from rural backgrounds. Three contributed and funded both. The same approach later brought in outside trainers and competition judges.

Working through ambiguity

At Grant Thornton the team wanted a view on plywood and laminates with no defined target list. I screened 721 building-materials deals to see where activity actually was, built a 55-company landscape with spread financials, and derived revenue per sheet to separate premium from commodity brands. The screen showed activity was concentrated in cement, which changed the team's approach.

Persuading with data

As CFO in Brighton College's Entrepreneurship Challenge, I built a two-year cash-flow model, cost analysis and pricing strategy for an eco-friendly product and presented it in a ten-minute pitch that led with unit economics. The team won £3,333.

Learning quickly

Butler Bets needed a pricing engine that would work with a few dozen users. I read up on Hanson's LMSR, worked through the cost function, and implemented it in TypeScript against Supabase so each trade updates the price. Eight markets and five leagues are now live in beta.

Research

As a Laidlaw Scholar I surveyed 36 Indian MSMEs on bank versus non-bank lending and interviewed a business owner for a case study. Bank borrowers more often added jobs, while non-banks won on speed and paperwork. The finding that the two channels serve different firms is the basis of my current write-up.
