How I work

How I tend to work.

A few moments that say more than a bullet point.

01Find the metric behind the metricGrant Thornton

The usual metrics were not enough, so I combined capacity and utilization to estimate revenue per sheet. It gave us a better way to compare positioning across peers.

02Own the bug when users find itButler Bets

A small beta broke the login flow. I traced the issue through deployment logs, fixed it, and tightened school-email access after the security review.

03Change your view when the data disagreesLaidlaw

My Laidlaw results did not fully match my hypothesis. I changed the framing instead of forcing the data to fit the question I started with.

04Get the resource, not just the ideaUtopia

For Utopia, I learned that a good idea is not enough. We had to call businesses, find trainers, raise money and work with schools to actually make things happen.
