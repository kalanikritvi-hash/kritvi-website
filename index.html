01Find the metric behind the metricGrant Thornton

Company revenue alone was not explaining peer differences, so I combined installed capacity and utilization to estimate revenue per sheet. The roughly 2× spread helped distinguish premium-product mix from commodity scale.

02Own the bug when users find itButler Bets

During a small beta, simultaneous sign-ins exposed a login-route failure. I traced the issue through deployment logs, fixed the backend flow and tightened school-email access after security review.

03Change your view when the data disagreesLaidlaw

I expected faster non-bank credit to translate more clearly into stronger growth. My survey results were more mixed. I kept the analysis centered on what respondents actually reported rather than forcing the original hypothesis.

04Get the resource, not just the ideaUtopia

For Utopia, we moved beyond programming to outreach and fundraising: contacting businesses, trainers and school stakeholders to secure support for books, infrastructure and activities rather than waiting for ideal conditions.
